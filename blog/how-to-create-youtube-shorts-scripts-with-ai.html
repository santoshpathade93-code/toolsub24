How to Create YouTube Shorts Scripts with AI

Published: 24 November 2026 · Video AI Workflow Guide

AI can help you move from a rough video idea to a usable YouTube Shorts script faster. The best result is not a generic paragraph. It is a short plan with a strong opening, clear scene beats, spoken lines, on-screen text and a useful call to action.

YouTube’s official creator guide describes Shorts as short-form vertical videos and recommends getting to the point quickly. It also notes that creators can use features such as text overlays, captions, music and in-app editing. Use those ideas as production choices, not as a promise of views.

Start with the viewer, not the tool

Before opening an AI writing or video tool, decide who should watch the Short and what they should understand by the end. A student tutorial, a local business offer and a Marathi recipe video need different vocabulary, pacing and examples.

Audience: Marathi-speaking college students Topic: Three free AI tools for making study notes Goal: Help viewers choose one tool to try today Language: Simple spoken Marathi with familiar English tool names Length: A concise 30–40 second script Tone: Helpful, energetic and not overhyped

The five-part Shorts script structure

1. Hook

The first line should make the topic clear or create a useful question. Avoid vague openings such as “Hello everyone, welcome back”. Try a specific result, problem or curiosity gap that the video can actually answer.

2. Context

Give one sentence that tells viewers why the topic matters. Keep it connected to the audience’s real situation.

3. Main beats

Break the idea into two or three visual steps. Each beat should say what the viewer sees and what the creator says. This makes the script easier to record and edit.

4. Proof or example

Show a real example, screen action or before-and-after comparison. Do not ask AI to invent statistics, customer results or product features.

5. CTA

End with one natural action, such as saving the video, trying a step or reading a related guide. One clear CTA is usually easier to follow than several requests.

A reusable AI prompt for Shorts

Clear instructions, relevant context, examples and a specified output format make AI drafts easier to control. This approach is consistent with the prompting guidance from OpenAI and Google.

You are a short-form video scriptwriter. Create a YouTube Shorts script about: [TOPIC] Audience: [WHO WILL WATCH] Language: [PURE MARATHI / MARATHI-ENGLISH / HINGLISH / ENGLISH] Target length: [SECONDS] Tone: [TONE] Return exactly: 1. Hook 2. Spoken script in short lines 3. Visual or screen action for each line 4. On-screen text 5. One CTA Rules: - Use only the facts supplied below. - Do not invent statistics, prices, features or testimonials. - Keep the wording natural when read aloud. - Make the first line specific and useful. Context: [YOUR VERIFIED FACTS]

Use AI video prompts scene by scene

If you are also generating visuals or B-roll, do not give the video model only a topic. Describe the subject, action, camera framing, visual style and sound when relevant. Google’s Veo prompt guide recommends detailed descriptions and shows how dialogue, audio cues and play-by-play action can make a scene more controllable.

Create a realistic vertical 9:16 B-roll shot for a Marathi study-tips Short. Subject: a college student organizing notes on a laptop. Action: the student highlights three headings and checks a simple list. Camera: medium close-up with a slow gentle push-in. Style: bright natural desk light, clean modern study space. Audio: soft keyboard taps, no spoken dialogue, no brand logos. Keep the screen text minimal and readable.
Important: AI-generated video may contain visual, text, voice or factual errors. Review every clip before publishing. Check names, numbers, subtitles, pronunciation, music rights and any claims about a product or service.

Editing checklist before upload

  • Does the opening line explain the topic quickly?
  • Can every spoken line be read naturally in one breath?
  • Does each script beat have a matching visual action?
  • Are captions large enough and away from important screen controls?
  • Is the video framed vertically for mobile viewing?
  • Did I verify names, dates, prices, features and statistics?
  • Did I use music, images and clips that I am allowed to use?
  • Is the title clear, and is there only one main CTA?

Frequently asked questions

Can AI write a complete YouTube Short?

AI can draft the hook, spoken lines, visual beats and CTA. You still need to add your own experience, verify facts, record or select suitable visuals and edit the final video.

Should Marathi Shorts use English words?

Use the language your audience naturally understands. A prompt can specify pure Marathi, spoken Marathi with familiar English terms, or Hinglish. Read the result aloud and remove words that sound forced.

Can AI guarantee more views?

No. A script can improve clarity and preparation, but views depend on many factors such as audience fit, topic, execution, distribution and viewer response. Do not publish claims that guarantee virality or income.

What is the best length for a Short?

YouTube’s official guide says Shorts are capped at three minutes. For a small tutorial, choose a length that fits the idea rather than stretching it. A concise script with one clear outcome is easier to record and edit.
